About B-Health Solutions

Your partner in health, wherever you are

B-Health Solutions LLC is a patient-centered primary care practice providing convenient, compassionate healthcare via telemedicine, in patients’ homes, and in group homes or facilities.

Request an Appointment Contact Us
Our Mission

Making quality healthcare more accessible

Our goal is to make quality healthcare more accessible — especially for older adults, individuals with mobility limitations, and patients who benefit from receiving care in the comfort of their own home.

Our services are provided by experienced healthcare professionals with an emphasis on prevention, chronic disease management, patient education, continuity of care, and individualized treatment plans. That means less time navigating waiting rooms and more time receiving care that is thoughtful, unhurried, and centered on you.

Our practice is led by Edna Badjie, FNP-BC, a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner with more than 16 years of healthcare experience. Meet Edna →

Who We Serve

  • Older adults seeking convenient, unhurried care
  • Individuals with mobility limitations
  • Patients who benefit from care at home
  • Residents of group homes & facilities

Edna Badjie, FNP-BC, is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner with over 16 years of healthcare experience, including extensive experience caring for patients in both skilled nursing and long-term care settings.

Edna is a graduate of an accredited Family Nurse Practitioner program and brings a wealth of clinical experience to her practice. Her background caring for individuals with complex and chronic health conditions has shaped her approach to providing thoughtful, comprehensive, and individualized care.

She believes quality healthcare begins with listening.

Edna takes the time to understand each patient’s concerns, health history, and personal goals while working collaboratively with patients, families, caregivers, and other members of the healthcare team.

Clinical Services

Primary & Preventive Care Chronic Disease Management Senior Care House Calls Telemedicine Foot Care Sleep Health Remote Patient Monitoring Diagnostic Services

Whether providing care in the clinic, through telemedicine, or in the comfort of a patient’s home, Edna’s goal is to make healthcare accessible, compassionate, and patient-centered — ensuring every patient feels heard, respected, and actively involved in their care.

What Guides Our Care

Five principles behind every visit

Prevention

Catching small concerns early with routine evaluations and proactive screening.

Chronic Disease Management

Ongoing support for hypertension, diabetes, cholesterol, thyroid, and other common conditions.

Patient Education

Clear, unhurried explanations so you understand your health and your options.

Continuity of Care

A consistent provider who knows your history — visit after visit.

Individualized Treatment

Care plans built around your specific health goals — not a one-size-fits-all approach.

How We Deliver Care

Three convenient ways to be seen

Via Telemedicine

Secure virtual visits for follow-ups, medication management, and appropriate concerns.

In Patients’ Homes

House-call visits for those who have difficulty traveling to a medical office.

Group Homes & Facilities

On-site visits for residents of group homes and assisted-living communities.
