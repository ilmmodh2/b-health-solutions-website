Getting Started

Yes. We welcome new patients. You can request your first appointment through our New Patients page, or call our office at 469-648-3475 Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM.

Complete the short form on our New Patients page with your contact information and preferred visit type, and our office will call or email you to confirm the details. You're always welcome to call us directly instead.

Have a photo ID and insurance card ready (if applicable), a current list of your medications and dosages, and any symptoms, concerns, or questions you'd like to discuss. It also helps to let us know whether you prefer telemedicine, a house call, or an in-office visit.

Our primary care services are provided for adults. If you're unsure whether we can help with your situation, please call our office and we'll point you in the right direction.

Insurance & Billing

We accept Medicare, Medicaid, BCBS, Molina Healthcare, UHC, Cigna, Superior HealthPlan, Ambetter, Wellcare, Aetna Better Health, and WellMed. You can see the full list on our New Patients page.

Please call our office at 469-648-3475. We'll help you confirm your coverage and review self-pay and payment options with you.

Coverage for Remote Patient Monitoring varies by plan and clinical situation. Our office will review your specific coverage with you before enrolling you in the program.

Our Services

We help patients manage hypertension, diabetes, high cholesterol, thyroid disorders, and other common medical conditions, with an emphasis on prevention, monitoring, and individualized treatment plans.

RPM lets us follow health measurements such as blood pressure, blood glucose, weight, and other clinically appropriate readings from your home. Our care team reviews the readings you transmit to help identify changes, support chronic disease management, and coordinate timely follow-up. Learn more on our Remote Patient Monitoring page.

We offer ABI/vascular screening, TM-Flow autonomic function testing, specimen collection, laboratory testing, and other diagnostic testing as clinically indicated. See our Diagnostic Services page for details.

Your provider reviews your health history and goals, then discusses an individualized plan that may include nutrition and lifestyle guidance, ongoing progress monitoring, and coordination with your other care. What's appropriate differs from person to person, so it's discussed during your consultation.

We evaluate symptoms that may indicate a sleep disorder — such as loud snoring, excessive daytime sleepiness, witnessed pauses in breathing, morning headaches, and poor-quality sleep. When clinically appropriate, eligible patients may be referred or coordinated for diagnostic sleep testing, with follow-up to review results.
