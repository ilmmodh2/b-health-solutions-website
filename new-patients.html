What to Expect

From request to your first visit

Submit Your Request

Complete the form below with your contact information and preferred visit type.

Our Office Confirms Details

We’ll call or email to confirm your appointment time, visit type, and answer any questions — including insurance and payment.

Attend Your First Visit

Meet your provider via telemedicine, at home, or in the office, and begin your individualized care plan.

Good to Know

Preparing for your first appointment

  • Have a photo ID and insurance card (if applicable) ready
  • Prepare a current list of medications and dosages
  • Note any symptoms, concerns, or questions you’d like to discuss
  • Let us know your preferred visit type: telemedicine, house call, or in-office

We accept most major insurance plans — see the full list below. Our office will also review self-pay and payment options with you directly when confirming your appointment.
