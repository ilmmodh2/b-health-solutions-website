Our Services

Comprehensive care, delivered your way

From ongoing primary care and house calls to remote monitoring, diagnostic testing, and weight management — every offering is designed around prevention, education, and convenience.

Primary Care

Routine evaluations, preventive care, medication management, and chronic disease management for hypertension, diabetes, cholesterol, and more.

Learn more

House Calls

In-home primary care for patients who have difficulty traveling — assessments, follow-ups, and medication reviews at home or in a group home setting.

Learn more

Telemedicine

Convenient virtual visits for appropriate concerns, follow-ups, medication management, and reviewing test or lab results.

Learn more

Remote Patient Monitoring

Ongoing monitoring of blood pressure, blood glucose, weight, and other measurements from home, reviewed by our care team.

Learn more

Foot Care

Routine foot care for older adults and patients with chronic conditions — assessments, nail care, and preventive education.

Learn more

Sleep Study Services

Evaluation for snoring, daytime sleepiness, and signs of sleep apnea, with coordination for diagnostic testing and follow-up.

Learn more

Diagnostic Services

ABI/vascular screening, TM-Flow autonomic function testing, specimen collection, and laboratory testing as clinically indicated.

Weight Loss Management
